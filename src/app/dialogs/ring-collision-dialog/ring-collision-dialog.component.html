What does this mean?

These aren't real physics events. The stellar forge is programmed to prevent collisions, but a few slip through. When two objects are on a collision course, they simply pass straight through each other and carry on in their orbits.

Overlap is the depth of the intersection at closest approach, as a percentage of the two objects' combined extents: 0% means the surfaces merely graze, 100% means the centres pass through one another. Rings are treated as a shell around their host rather than a flat disc, so this flags whether the objects can physically coincide, not the finer detail of exactly which part of the ring they cross.

A real ring is a thin disc tilted to its host's own equator, not a shell — a tilt this model has no data to measure. Where the predicted window is a close graze, that unmodelled tilt can in practice split it into two shorter collisions, separated by a brief gap right at closest approach, rather than one continuous one.
