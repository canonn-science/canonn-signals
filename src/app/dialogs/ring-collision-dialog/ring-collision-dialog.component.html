What does this mean?

These aren't real physics events. The stellar forge is programmed to prevent collisions, but a few slip through. When two objects are on a collision course, they simply pass straight through each other and carry on in their orbits.

This is a simplified radial-band check, not a full 3D simulation of the ring's plane — it treats the ring as a shell around its host rather than a flat disc, so it flags whether the objects can coincide at all, and (when the pair's orbits share a single frame) roughly when.
