How the influencing star shapes biology

A body's biological signals aren't decided by its own properties alone — the class, temperature and luminosity of its governing star are part of what determines which genera and species of life can spawn, alongside the body's own temperature, gravity, atmosphere and volcanism. Many genera and species are only ever found around specific star classes or ranges of stellar temperature, so identifying the influencing star narrows down which biology is plausible on this body before it's even been scanned.
