How this works

Every gas giant is drawn with seven cloud layers, each coloured from a chart of temperature bands. A layer exactly on the boundary between two bands matches neither, gets no colour, and keeps the renderer's default: pure green. That is a Green Gas Giant.

The layers are evenly spaced from the surface temperature up to the lower of two limits: how far they can reach, set by surface temperature and bulk density, or a fixed ceiling for the temperature band. Some classes also keep the spacing within a fixed range.

The match must be exact to about seven significant digits: a layer the smallest possible gap off a boundary renders normally. Some classes also get a random temperature offset first, which no public data records, so for those the model can only narrow it down.

Cloud-layer model by CMDRs Arcanic and Regza, Green Gas Giant catalogue by CMDR Arcanic — how a GGG happens.
